# Privacy Policy — Open Tab on the Right

_Last updated: 2026-07-06_

Open Tab on the Right does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or web page content.

## What the extension stores

The extension uses the browser's local `storage` permission solely to remember two on/off preferences you set yourself:

- **Tab wrapping** — whether moving a tab past the end of the tab bar wraps to the other side.
- **Open as child tab** (Firefox only) — whether new tabs nest under the tab that opened them.

These values are stored locally on your device via the browser's built-in storage API. They are never transmitted anywhere, never leave your device, and are not accessible to the developer or any third party.

## What the extension does not do

- No content scripts run on any web page you visit.
- No browsing history, page content, or personal information is read, collected, or transmitted.
- No analytics, tracking, or third-party services are used.
- No network requests are made by the extension.

## Permissions

The extension requests only the `storage` permission, used exclusively for the preferences described above.
